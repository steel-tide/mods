# Cyberpunk: Blackout Protocol — sprite workbook

Original art, generated through Steel Tide's existing `game/core/scripts/gen-sprites.mjs` with `openai/gpt-image-2`, quality high. Masters are kept in `art/sprites/`; shipping cuts are built by `cut-art.mjs` with the vanilla headroom/supersampling recipe. No vanilla files are written.

The faction's material language is graphite ceramic, cyan optics, copper bus bars and magenta recolour panels. Each unit has a different silhouette. All references, when used, are our own generated masters.
