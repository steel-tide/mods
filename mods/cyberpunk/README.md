# Cyberpunk: Blackout Protocol

An official Steel Tide expansion set in the fractured industrial city of Veyra. Graphite armor, cyan optics and copper power systems give a new arsenal its own silhouette and sound.

**17 building types + 7 upgrades · 32 recruitable units · 8 battlefields · 24 optional mastery challenges**

The roster combines regenerating shields, a shared fire-control network, stealth raiders, deployable mass drivers, airlift operations and autonomous carrier wings. Photon weapons draw native laser beams and strike at beam speed; their shots cannot be intercepted. The Arc Strider uses a jagged electrical beam with area damage. These are functional weapons, not recolored bullet sprites.

## Play

1. Open [Cyberpunk in Steel Tide](https://play.steelti.de/?mod=cyberpunk), or find **Cyberpunk: Blackout Protocol** in **Settings → Mods → Official registry** and install it. For an offline installer, download `cyberpunk-1.2.1.zip` from the [1.2.1 release](https://github.com/steel-tide/mods/releases/tag/cyberpunk-1.2.1), then use **Upload file…** with the ZIP intact.
2. Start a skirmish, choose **Blackout Protocol** in each desired seat’s **Faction** field, and choose **01 · Gridfall** from the mod’s maps. This version needs a Steel Tide build with mod format 2 support.
3. For the operation atlas, use **Standard**, fog on, **1,600 starting metal**, **150 population**, **6 production slots per HQ**, and turn **Build radius** off. The atlas's island outposts assume unrestricted construction. The arsenal also works with normal construction limits in ordinary matches.
4. Play maps 01–06 as a duel. On 07 and 08, put **seats 1 + 2 on one team** and **3 + 4 on the other**. Your partner may be an AI or another player.
5. Open **FIELD-MANUAL.html** from the extracted ZIP for the operation briefings, full roster, tactical roles and a locally saved challenge checklist. It works offline in English, Chinese and Korean.

On any ordinary map, selecting **Blackout Protocol** starts you with a **Backstreet Nexus** and two **Fabricators**. Use a Fabricator to build a **Chassis Foundry**. The **Vector Skyport** follows the Foundry. The **Signal Spire** opens advanced ground, air and naval equipment. The **Oracle Core** opens heavy walkers and capital ships. The **Undertide Dock** is the naval production line. The Foundry, Skyport and Dock use the normal HQ production slots; expand your headquarters or adjust the match's slot setting when you need more lines.

The Nexus is your headquarters: it supplies starting power, income, territory and production slots, and produces Fabricators and transportable Stitch construction rigs. Fabricators build and rebuild the Cyberpunk base. You can field Blackout Protocol against Steel Tide or another Blackout Protocol seat without vanilla headquarters or engineers. Everyone in a multiplayer room must have the exact mod version required by the host.

## Standalone faction and vanilla opponents

For a normal 1v1, choose a regular map such as Greenfield or Rift Valley, use the same starting metal and population for both seats, select Blackout Protocol for yourself and Steel Tide for the opponent. The authored operation maps intentionally contain scenario forces and are not equal-start balance arenas.

- **Complete economy:** land and offshore extraction, three mining levels, three power levels, repair and headquarters expansion.
- **Combined arms:** Needle and Sentinel open the ground line; Lancers counter armor, Wardens and Palisades cover aircraft, and Firewall escorts intercept missiles. Support units do not replace combat troops.
- **Siege and counterplay:** Breach Spires, deployed Crawlers and Longbows break fortifications. Void Batteries fire interceptable siege missiles. Native laser beams remain uninterceptable; they still need sight and suitable targets.
- **Logistics:** Stitch builders ride Harriers or Splice Landing Barges. Fabricators cross water themselves. Tempests carry two bombing runs and return to a powered Skyport to rearm.
- **Strategic defense:** Aurora Arrays, Palisades and Aegir escorts engage nuclear warheads without veterancy. Null Chapels jam radar and remote cameras, while visual scouting and sonar remain effective.

Construction follows the match's territory rule. On default settings, extend headquarters coverage before placing distant permanent structures; deposits remain exempt. A surviving transported builder can rebuild a lost headquarters.

The [matchup guide](BALANCE.md) explains each strength and its counterplay. The current stock AI is less effective with Nexus naval logistics and shield rotation; competitive human parity is still unmeasured.

## Two tours of Veyra

The eight maps have different openings and terrain problems: a disadvantaged urban opening, three contested crossings, a dense market, a naval channel, a volcanic siege, a mountain airlift, a two-front alliance and a fortified final offensive. They are reusable skirmish battlefields with authored starting forces. The standard match victory rules apply.

The manual's 24 mastery goals are **optional, player-tracked challenges**. A first tour and a second tour on Seasoned with the production restrictions provide the intended long-form experience. A full two-tour run is designed around **10+ hours**, including learning and retries; this is a design target, not a measured playtime guarantee. Individual battle lengths depend on difficulty and play style.

## Included

- Seventeen placeable building types, seven economic/air-defense upgrades and thirty-two recruitable units. The deployed artillery form and carrier-only drone are counted separately.
- Seventy-seven original body, turret, projectile and effect sheets. All twenty-four building definitions have animated power systems; five walkers have registered movement frames.
- Twelve original weapon and machinery sounds, and three cover paintings. The registry opens with the Blackout cover illustration, followed by four gameplay screenshots.
- Eight maps, four gameplay screenshots, three-language names/descriptions/ability text, and an offline field manual.

The published folder includes the art prompt workbooks. Full-resolution masters and authoring tools remain in the authoring workspace; the installer contains shipping assets and player documentation.
