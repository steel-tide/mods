## Track 1 — `blackout-circuit.mp3` · “Blackout Circuit”

Original instrumental background music for the industrial city of Veyra.
One 120-second take through ElevenLabs `music_v2`, `force_instrumental: true`,
master format `mp3_44100_192`. Preserve the response in
`art/music/blackout-circuit.mp3`; derive the 128 kbps shipping copy at
`music/blackout-circuit.mp3` from that master.

```text
Compose an original 120-second instrumental cyberpunk real-time strategy game background score titled Blackout Circuit. A fractured industrial city at night: graphite-armored machines, cyan optics, copper power conduits, rain-dark streets, a resistance grid quietly coming alive. Controlled tactical urgency rather than panic or spectacle.

112 BPM, steady 4/4, D minor with occasional suspended and Dorian color. A warm, rounded analog bass pulse anchors a dry electronic kick and restrained industrial percussion: muted metal taps, clipped rim clicks, soft syncopated hi-hats. Add a short, memorable four-note synth motif, glassy arpeggio fragments and wide, low-lit pads. Texture should feel electrical and mechanical but remain musical, with no literal combat sound effects. Keep the bass articulate and the high frequencies smooth. Leave space in the middle for game voices and weapons.

Start with an audible bass pulse and a sparse hint of the motif in the first two seconds. Introduce the rhythm gradually over the first eight bars. Develop in connected sixteen-bar phrases, alternating a slightly fuller tactical groove with thinner atmospheric passages. Around the middle, briefly pull the percussion back while the bass and pads hold the pulse, then bring the groove back with a small melodic variation. Maintain moderate intensity throughout so this works in a command menu as well as beneath a long battle. Finish with a short deliberate musical resolution and a natural two-second tail, not an abrupt cutoff. The piece must feel complete at two minutes.

Entirely instrumental. No singing, speech, chanting, choir or vocal chops. No trailer booms, risers, huge drops, orchestral fanfare, distorted lead guitar, piercing alarms or long silence. No imitation of any named artist, franchise or existing soundtrack. Original music only, spacious stereo production with restrained dynamics and clean headroom.
```
