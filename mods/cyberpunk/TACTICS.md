# Cyberpunk — field tactics

Veyra was built around a privately owned power grid. When the exchanges sealed their districts, the city split into islands of light. A coalition of dock crews, machine tenders and disconnected neighbourhoods has seized an abandoned Nexus. The Blackout Protocol is their plan to take the exchanges apart, district by district, and reopen the city.

## The networked formation

Build an Aegis screen with Lancers behind it, a Conductor behind those, and a Suture off the direct firing line. Linked shooters have a 16% shorter reload interval inside the network. Multiple relays do not stack. Runner, Mantis, Aegis and Wraith do not receive that reload pulse; their distinct abilities keep their own behavior.

Rotate damaged shields out of contact. Shield delay counts time without taking damage; Suture recovery counts time without firing. These are separate conditions. Hold fire can trade immediate damage for recovery, but incoming damage can still overwhelm it.

Use detector raids to open a route for Wraiths, airlift to bypass long ground corridors, and split naval escorts between sonar and air defense. A mixed formation has practical advantages over repeating one expensive chassis.

## Blackout District

Blackout District rises in stepped towers, slab blocks and terraces around narrow service courts. Roof machinery, layered facade panels and cyan, amber and coral commercial signs crowd its graphite streets. City movement, road access and destruction follow the usual rules. This district requires Steel Tide 0.8.15; ordinary maps keep their own city appearance.

## Roster

### Backstreet Nexus
Headquarters, construction production and territory anchor. Its prism emitter fends off early raids; rebuild through a Fabricator or a transported Stitch.

### Substrate Tap
Place on a land deposit. Expansion income can fund replacement screens while your specialists survive.

### Flux Reactor
An economical first power source, with two upgrade levels. Leave space between reactors and workshops: a destroyed core damages nearby friends.

### Chassis Foundry
The main ground production line. Spire and Oracle prerequisites gate advanced chassis individually.

### Signal Spire
Unlocks advanced ground and aircraft production. Reveals stealth and supports linked shooters within six tiles.

### Vector Skyport
Reconnaissance, interception, plasma strike and cargo in one production line. Escort transports before loading expensive units.

### Undertide Dock
Build along a shoreline. Skiffs and submarines screen the Oracle-gated capital ships.

### Prism Bastion
A shielded direct-fire beam emplacement. Aircraft and artillery can attack beyond its response envelope.

### Aegis Lattice
Repairs three nearby targets and intercepts eligible projectiles. It cannot intercept a laser beam.

### Oracle Core
Unlocks heavy walkers and capital ships. It also supplies a local fire-control link; protect its substantial power demand.

### Splice Fabricator
Amphibious construction and repair. Builds every Cyberpunk structure, including replacement and expansion headquarters.

### Glitch Runner
Fast amphibious reconnaissance with close detection. Slipstream is a retreat or flank tool that increases incoming damage.

### Needle Automaton
Anti-light screen and short-range drone defense. Heavy armor and longer-range cannons punish an unsupported Needle line. Benefits from the relay network.

### Mantis Skirmisher
Mobile plasma bursts with area damage. Redline shortens reloads for seven seconds but increases damage taken; pull back when the burst ends.

### Aegis Screen Tank
Soaks fire while Lancers shoot from behind. Lock Shields reduces movement to 35% and incoming damage to 55%; rotate damaged hulls out to recharge.

### Photon Lancer
Long-range anti-armor beam with a close blind zone. Stop at standoff range; an unsupported attack-move lets tanks close on its light hull.

### Suture Tender
An active repair vehicle and a passive recovery anchor. Nearby friendly Cyberpunk units heal after four seconds without firing. Multiple Sutures do not multiply that pulse.

### Null Wraith
Stealth flank attacker. Targets exposed Lancers, relays and workshops; a kill restores some hull integrity. Detectors and massed Needles counter it.

### Breach Crawler
Deploy into long-range area artillery; pack before retreating. It automatically deploys when stationary near an enemy. Its blast can damage friendly units.

### Conductor Relay
Mobile detection and shared fire control. Keep it behind the screen; losing it removes the reload benefit from linked units after a short grace period.

### Arc Strider
Shielded electrical area damage against clustered light and medium forces. Long-range anti-tank weapons and aircraft punish its slow approach.

### Archon Siege Engine
Heavy siege beam optimized for structures, with a minimum range. Three may stand at once. It needs anti-air cover and a screen against rushing tanks.

### Kite Survey Drone
Unarmed flying reconnaissance and stealth detection. Spots for deployed guns and reveals infiltration routes; keep clear of interceptors.

### Razor Interceptor
Dedicated anti-air laser fighter. Clears Vespers and carrier drones but contributes no ground damage.

### Vesper Gunship
Hovering plasma strike aircraft. Punishes exposed ground formations; Razors and sustained anti-air fire drive it away.

### Harrier Dropship
Shielded airlift with ten cargo capacity. Carries ground combat units across ridges and water; Fabricators cross water on their own. Amphibious units cannot board; it has no weapon.

### Prism Skiff
Fast laser patrol and sonar escort. Its anti-sub torpedoes work at screening range; keep enough hulls around a valuable carrier.

### Undertow Hunter
Submerged fleet hunter with long sonar reach. Deep water hides it from surface-only weapons; shallows force it up where railguns can hit it.

### Ferryman Drone Tender
Autonomous five-drone strike wing. Lost drones rebuild one at a time; returning aircraft rearm aboard. Escort against submarines and interceptors.

### Leviathan Rail Cruiser
Fore-and-aft rail mounts bombard ships and shore. Turning the hull changes which gun bears. Submarines and aircraft exploit its missing defenses.

### Substrate Tap II
Upgrade a working Tap for stronger income without claiming another deposit. Budget for the additional grid load.

### Substrate Tap III
Deep extraction supports a sustained late-game army. Its expensive upgrade makes exposed forward deposits a risky investment.

### Flux Reactor II
The intermediate grid upgrade keeps specialist production and weapons supplied without spreading out more fragile plants.

### Flux Reactor III
Dense power for an Oracle district or naval base. Losing one can brown out several workshops; a reserve grid keeps repair and interception working.

### Brine Tap
The Fabricator swims out to build on offshore ore. Stitch rigs cannot work at sea; keep a Skiff nearby to watch for submarines.

### Brine Tap 2
A second-stage offshore economy. The platform remains a stationary target, so escort spending matters as much as the upgrade.

### Brine Tap 3
High-output seabed extraction rewards control of the shipping lanes. Repair and anti-air cover help preserve the investment.

### Halo Battery
Early stationary air defence without a Signal Spire. It cannot shoot ground troops; pair it with a Bastion. Upgrade to Aurora for strategic interception.

### Aurora Array
Anti-air and nuclear interception unlocked through Halo and the Signal Spire. Spread arrays along a missile corridor; a last-second cluster over the target is less reliable.

### Null Chapel
Jams enemy radar and remote camera feeds, including Kites. Visual scouts and sonar still work. The field needs full power and exposes the transmitter location.

### Reboot Bay
An affordable repair point before the Signal Spire. Rotate damaged screens through it. Repair consumes metal, and the bay does not intercept missiles.

### Breach Spire
Static area artillery outranges ordinary turrets. Its minimum range and friendly splash make a close defensive screen essential.

### Tidewatch Pylon
Offshore torpedo defence against surface ships and detected submarines. It has no sonar of its own: a Skiff or Undertow must reveal submerged targets.

### Void Battery
Long-range siege missiles pressure entrenched bases. Interceptors can shoot the missiles down; screen the minimum range and avoid firing over your own assault troops.

### Stitch Construction Rig
Ground construction that fits a Harrier or landing barge. Builds the land roster, including the Nexus. Ordinary headquarters territory rules still govern distant construction.

### Sentinel Line Tank
A mobile shield tank that fires while withdrawing. Its light hull and medium armor lose prolonged frontal trades against cannon tanks; use reach, speed and shield recovery to cycle it out of danger.

### Warden Flak Rig
Mobile early anti-air. It cannot damage tanks or buildings; keep it behind the fighting line. Its flak splash punishes closely packed aircraft.

### Palisade SAM Carrier
Long-range mobile air cover with nuclear interception. The missiles are interceptable, and its light chassis needs ground escorts.

### Firewall Escort
A moving interception magazine for armoured columns. Sustained missile salvos can exhaust it; lasers and artillery shells bypass its defence.

### Longbow Siege Carrier
Mobile siege with a long minimum range. Use scouting to hit fortifications before advancing. Unlike lasers, its missiles can be intercepted.

### Locust Breach Drone
A fragile, fast breaching charge that needs an explicit attack target. Limited to ten; low-altitude flight exposes it to roof guns and flak.

### Tempest Bomber
Two bomb runs, then an automatic return to a Vector Skyport for rearming. Escort the approach and keep the home grid alive; bombers cannot fight aircraft.

### Aegir Escort Frigate
Radar, missile air defence and nuclear interception for the fleet. Its surface gun is a secondary armament; protect it from submarines with sonar escorts.

### Splice Landing Barge
Moves ground troops and Stitch builders across water. Approach the last navigable water tile before unloading on a coast. It cannot carry amphibious Fabricators.

### Cutlass Missile Corvette
A long-range anti-ship specialist. Kite conventional gunships, but expect point defence to blunt the salvo. It has no weapon against land, aircraft or submarines.

### Ravager Amphibious Tank
Crosses rivers with its own armour and plasma gun. It changes target domain afloat; torpedoes become a threat, and a landing barge is unnecessary.

## Operation atlas

Use Standard, fog on, 1,600 metal, 150 population, six production slots per HQ and Build radius off. Maps 07–08 use seats 1+2 versus 3+4. These are skirmishes; extra mastery goals are self-tracked. Estimated operation times are planning targets, not measured playtime.

### 01 · Gridfall
The exchange quarter has gone dark. Your workshops still run, but the opposing district already has a working signal network. The central switching yards are the quickest source of metal and the first place both sides will meet.

Expand into the middle, field Needles against light raiders, and use Mantis Redline only while you can disengage. Build the Spire before committing to shield armor. A Suture lets a damaged force withdraw and return.

Mastery challenges:

- Take two central deposits before destroying an enemy production building.
- Win without constructing an Oracle Core.
- Win on Seasoned using only Cyberpunk combat production.

### 02 · Glass Causeway
The reservoir gates have been welded shut. Three causeways lead to the eastern factories. Their nearest quay is fortified, but every crossing leads to a different flank.

Place Aegis tanks ahead of Lancers. Lock Shields buys time at the crossing, then pull battered tanks behind the firing line to recharge. Relays belong close enough to help the Lancers but far enough back to survive. A second crossing can carry the decisive raid.

Mastery challenges:

- Cross and fight on all three causeways before winning.
- Win without losing either starting Aegis tank.
- Win on Seasoned without building artillery or Archons.

### 03 · Ghost Market
Behind the shuttered market, service alleys lead past the checkpoints. The exchange pays its sentries well; its tenders and relays are less secure. Your infiltration force begins with a dropship and two Wraiths.

Scout detector positions before committing Wraiths. A Wraith excels inside a Lancer's minimum range, but loses quickly to concentrated Needles. Use the ring road to distract the main force while a small group raids a different entrance.

Mastery challenges:

- Destroy a Conductor or Spire with Wraiths before your first frontal base attack.
- Win without losing either starting Wraith.
- Win on Seasoned without producing Aegis, Strider or Archon units.

### 04 · Undertow
The shipping channel is the city's remaining supply artery. A sand island divides the deep lanes. The eastern fleet brings a rail cruiser and a drone tender; your submarines can contest the depths, but the shore still has to be taken.

Prism Skiffs screen submerged hunters with sonar and close torpedoes. Undertows threaten unscreened carriers and cruisers. The Ferryman's wing attacks surface targets; it cannot solve a submarine attack. Land a Fabricator on the island to support an airlift across the channel.

Mastery challenges:

- Establish a Substrate Tap and a Prism Bastion on the central island.
- Win while preserving your starting Ferryman.
- Win on Seasoned without producing Leviathans or vanilla warships.

### 05 · Cinder Circuit
The furnace quarter feeds the exchange's armored reserve. Basalt walls hide gun positions, and the southern works have a walking siege engine. A direct advance meets overlapping artillery; the ash roads offer another approach.

Observe with a Kite or Runner, deploy Breach Crawlers behind the screen, and move friendly armor clear of their impact zone. Pack before changing front. Reactor ruptures punish dense construction on either side, making raiding as valuable as bombardment.

Mastery challenges:

- Win while keeping both starting Breach Crawlers alive.
- Destroy an enemy reactor with a raiding force before your first Archon is produced.
- Win on Seasoned without producing aircraft.

### 06 · Cloudbreak
Frozen ridgelines divide the northern service corridor. The ground route is long, but the clearings between the walls can hold a forward workshop. Your two dropships can put a raiding group behind the next ridge.

Send Razors ahead of loaded Harriers. Their shields buy a short margin under fire. Airlift ground escorts over the ridge while Fabricators take the land or water route to establish a forward Nexus. Amphibious Fabricators and Runners cannot ride in this ground-unit hold. Keep a retreat route for damaged aircraft.

Mastery challenges:

- Land ground units in the central clearing and hold a deposit there.
- Win while keeping both starting Harriers alive.
- Win on Seasoned without producing Vespers, Breach Crawlers or Archons.

### 07 · Split Signal
Two western districts have joined the coalition. The port now divides a city fighting on two fronts. A shared relay network can keep both armies moving, but concentrating everything on one avenue leaves the other district exposed.

Set seats 1 and 2 to the same team, against seats 3 and 4. Friendly relays and Suture support include allies. Keep a reserve between the two western fronts and use the port crossings to change where your opponent must defend.

Mastery challenges:

- Win with both allied headquarters still standing.
- Fight alongside your ally with a Conductor and a Suture from your own army.
- Win with both enemies on Seasoned; do not produce Archons.

### 08 · Blackout
The central exchange has closed its perimeter. Prism guns, deployed drivers and a heavy reserve protect the eastern districts. The reservoir divides the approaches; the outer streets run behind parts of the line. This is the coalition's final offensive.

Use the same 1+2 versus 3+4 teams. Scout before moving siege weapons into overlapping fields of fire. Raids remove relays and reactors while shielded formations pin the defenders. Split your support assets so one bombardment cannot break the whole army.

Mastery challenges:

- Destroy both enemy Oracle Cores before the final enemy headquarters falls.
- Win while preserving your starting Conductor and Suture.
- Win with both enemies on Seasoned and without producing an Archon.
