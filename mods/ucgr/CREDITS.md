The Steel Tide port adds the content manifest, importer, native faction
integration, Korean translations, compatibility audit and the new
**Crossroads Exercise** map. Original unit artwork is composited from the
source layers. Exported firing recordings come from the supplied package.
Version 0.5.0 also carries 41 original selection/order recordings and ten
original component-status PNGs in this pack's own extension assets. PNG bytes
are unchanged; voice recordings retain their decoded PCM16, original sample
rate and channels in portable WAV files. Their source paths and hashes are recorded in
[feedback-source-data.json](feedback-source-data.json). Commented-out voice
bindings remain silent. The full-spectrum mode's music remains deferred with
skipped M5; it is not repurposed as national-faction music. No new roster unit
was added with its defining reload or weapon-switching behavior missing.
These conversion contributions do not transfer ownership of the original
art, audio, writing or design.

Crossroads Exercise is an original map made for this MVP. None of the six
source 5v5 TMX scenarios is included as a converted scenario. The PPSH bonus
content remains deferred from the MVP roster. Its original
`未经允许禁止使用.txt` filename is recorded as source evidence; it is no longer
classified as a hold for missing studio permission.

UCGR owns and carries the `combined-arms` extension source, build, tests and
documentation in [`extensions/combined-arms-v1`](extensions/combined-arms-v1/README.md).
Its implementation is derived from Steel Tide engine commit `31095b4f` and retains the game package's declared
`PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0` license. It is separate from the original UCGR
assets and source material covered by the notices above.

The UCGR promotional cover (`screenshots/cover.jpg`) was created with the
built-in image generation tool from this port’s Combined Arms screenshot,
with title lettering and atmospheric treatment. It is promotional artwork;
the following gallery images are gameplay captures. The generation brief is
retained in `screenshots/cover-prompt.txt`. Original unit artwork remains
credited to its source creators above.
