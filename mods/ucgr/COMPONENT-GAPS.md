# UCGR component combat and logistics coverage

`component_port.py` and `logistics_port.py` compile the original pack's resolved data into this pack's own extension. They do not execute RW scripts or change the game. `combat-coverage.json` lists the source entries that pass the complete component compiler and the source component entries still outside its reviewed contracts. The roster remains a separate, explicit selection: a successful source preflight does not add a unit to the faction menu.

## Implemented source families

The reviewed contracts now cover the shared `tem/tank.key` / `模板/坦克模板.key` tank, IFV, recovery and open-compartment families, rather than only T-90M. Required damage/repair actions must retain their reviewed provenance. Source-specific globals supply plate/layer protection, sector angles, layout digits, component durability, reserve count and repair time.

- Finite main-gun inventories use the exact free ten-second `action_弹药补给`, one shared selectable feed and the real source stocks. AP, HE, HEAT and ATGM are enabled only when their actual helpers compile. The AM3 class carries its inherited HE stock without inventing an unavailable HE barrel. DTP-105 is recognized from its HEAT helper despite its HE inventory label.
- Vehicles without a main-round inventory use the source fixed rack: strict `damage > durability`, with no invented empty-rack allowance. BMP-2 has rack/driver durability 85/80; M2A3 has 90/20; BREM-1 has 10000/100. Their ordinary automatic magazines are independent of rack occupancy.
- `tem/ap.key` supplies current-shooter range, the source polynomial, jitter, incidence/ricochet checks, independent layout channels and zero hull damage against component armor. Rear impacts intentionally wear the side ERA layer, matching the source. Rank changes the source jitter lower bound and channel probability, including the source discontinuity at two armor kills.
- `tem/heat.key` keeps penetration and blast as separate effects: blocked penetration can still cause blast wounds. It preserves CE layer coefficients, residual penetration scaling, engine/track coefficients and the 80-roll accuracy weak-shot branch. `tem/tam.key` uses top armor and **front** layout exposure, including its separate engine/track damage scaling and unconditional reserve damage. These payload bindings compose with the separate guidance and source-projectile lifecycle modules.
- MG/LMG source tags compile their base-plate penetration and independent internal channels without consuming ERA, damaging reserve crew or interrupting repair. Ordinary damage uses source flat armor, armor-ignore, minimum retained damage and constant with/without-tag damage multipliers on compatible UCGR targets. Vanilla targets retain native armor tables. Unsupported armored component recipients retain their prior HP adaptation; their 100000 source armor is not enabled without their missing damage loop.
- Source helper recipient selection chooses one nearest enemy armored recipient within the authored range and radius test, instead of penetrating every native splash victim. HE keeps source friendly fire; HEAT keeps its ordinary enemy-only blast. HE pressure uses a radius floor only when `opeBatR == 1`; open compartments use the source unfloored distance.
- HE forest radius switching and the infantry damage reduction use native Forest tile centers as the port's environmental markers. The original RW map helper-entity positions and native terrain cells are different representations; this is not a claim of identical map geometry.
- Armor kills are credited at terminal component failure, before delayed destruction; air kills remain a separate counter. Morale recovery retains the inclusive source 61/81/101 ceilings, quiet/movement/combat conditions, the permanent steadfast latch, damage morale penalties and the below-20 firing gate. Rank, morale, movement, target size, range and forest contribute to the source accuracy value. Empty reserve applies the source +0.5 reload penalty only to manual-loader vehicles. Commander sight loss retains the source count threshold and latch behavior.
- Zero-initial-reserve source vehicles start with no reserve. Their source refill can add one count while retaining zero pooled health; the commander role follows the gunner seat. This source arithmetic is retained explicitly.
- Rack failure, exhausted crew, the 3.5-second delayed attributed death, reserve replacement, five-second abandonment, manual seat transfer and shared quantized repair are retained. Vacated seats gain ten damage beyond their own HP, including 20-HP crew. Rack repair can accumulate time above base HP without completing until its source completion condition becomes true. Reloaded stock rebases rack damage, including negative damage. The sampled rack-explosion hull value survives later damage and save/restore.
- Crew service uses each recipient's source duration: BMP-3/BRDM use 20 seconds instead of the usual 18. Original movement/fire service locks remain independent. Source automatic drivetrain recovery and manual work share one clock; penetrating hits retain `floor(0.5 * progress)`.
- Free special services retain their source duration and recipient stock mapping: artillery uses twenty seconds and SAM carriers sixty seconds where authored. HAWK and IRIS-T source display magazines retain their distinct initial counts and automatic cycles. TOS-1A carries its authored initial rockets and energy without inventing its paid resupply.
- Source APS mounts have separate loaded rounds, reserve counts, strict reload timers and a twenty-second combined stock/component refill. Their moving interceptors choose the last eligible unclaimed ATGM in creation order, preserve source acceleration and lifetime, and expire the intercepted projectile's life so its original expiry behavior still runs. The launcher's zero-reserve initial loaded-round quirk is retained.
- AP weak shots allocate a logical source helper identity at actual helper creation, then apply the original helper-ID parity expression. Native entity IDs are never consumed for invisible helpers. The logical namespace and surviving helper bindings are serialized; cross-engine ID values need not coincide, but no replacement random coin flip is used.
- Constant direct and area HP phases are additive and keep their own armor-ignore and tag mutators. Ordinary source area damage uses center distance and the original `min(1, 1.1 - distance/radius)` falloff; edge distance is used only when explicitly authored. Expanding waves retain one-hit membership and their full-radius falloff through save/restore.
- Constant multi-helper spawn lists retain separate child payloads and world-axis offsets, including HE/TAM pairs and the nineteen/twenty-four cluster bomblets. AP penetration emits six fragments, blocking emits four, and ricochet emits its own projectile; HEAT/TAM emit two. The first fragment creates its delayed child on its first active update. Native presentation resolves the corresponding original child variant instead of repeating the parent effect.
- Source non-ballistic projectile motion retains acceleration, turn rates, ground spread, delay, gravity displacement, vertical velocity, target-height steering and expiry. This includes direct turning AA rounds and artillery/fragment gravity paths. Ballistic-mode, retargeting and arbitrary conditional spawn expressions are not accepted by this compiler.
- Normal per-turret warmup is separate from reload. It advances at eligible aligned firing checks, fires on the update after reaching its threshold, resets after firing and decays at the source rate while all barrels are idle. All 1,973 audited source turrets use this reset mode; targeting queries do not spend warmup time. Private clocks survive save/restore.
- T-90M's five-armored-kill achievement converts the same vehicle to T-90M Arena-M. The conversion retains hull-health fraction, component wounds, spent ERA, crew identities, ammunition, kill counters, turret cooldowns and orders. Its completed tag belongs to one player, including when players are allied, and survives the winning vehicle's death and save/restore. The upgraded form adds its original finite APS. The localized achievement label is available in the unit inspection panel.
- Collision-free FPV sliding uses source float32 velocity, body angular acceleration and the original lookup-table trigonometry. Attack drones retain zero deceleration and coast. [MOVEMENT-PARITY.md](MOVEMENT-PARITY.md) records the inspected RW revision and the separate boundary for ordinary route/reverse decisions.
- Exact empty-magazine cycles, rifle idle partial-magazine discard and Korean queued partial reloads are compiled. Queued reloads retain live rounds and continue if combat starts. Automatic reload start/completion counters distinguish actual reload events from depot service.
- Aircraft retain finite initial gun, rocket and missile stocks without inventing free rearm. Attachment occupancy and the audited initial loadout actions produce MIG-29's four R73 + two R27T and F-2's four AIM120 + two AIM9M. Shared barrels consume one shared stock. Unresolved initial inventories cannot become unlimited weapons. Paid rearm/loadout purchases remain outside this pass.

T-90M source values remain useful regression anchors: KE plate 600/100/60, CE plate 650/150/60, KE layer 300/200/0, CE layer 700/700/0, sector half-angle 35 degrees, rack 175, other parts 100. Its 3BM60 helper uses strength 85, diameter .696 and penetration 700. Its 3OF26 helper yield is **1.73**, although the unit description says 5.24; the helper value is preserved.

## Remaining component/logistics gaps

These remain pack work with the available APIs; this document does not claim full source parity.

- Unsupported arbitrary expressions, conditional/chance-based helper lists and non-reviewed projectile families still require a source-specific compiler. Existing exact families are not evidence that an arbitrary RW script can execute. The sensor/flight module compiles additional guided-helper chains separately.
- Crew replacement/refill briefly resets source movement flags before wound triggers reapply them. The port preserves the stable component penalties, not that sub-tick transient. Full source action-message ordering remains a separate fidelity concern.
- APS's atmospheric smoke art is omitted where the original drag/wind behavior cannot yet be established. Its finite ammunition and interception behavior are active; an invented native explosion is not used as a substitute.
- Source paid rearm, arbitrary purchased loadouts and resource-linked achievements are still absent. Initial finite aircraft stock is implemented independently; it does not imply these purchases exist. M5's economy/mode work remains excluded.
- Reinforcement achievements remain with M5 even when they grant no numeric resource: their global tags are consumed by source spawn-point scripts. For example, T-72A's Soviet Steel tag spawns T-72AV, and the Dark Horse tag requests Leopard 2A5. The port does not replace these rewards with an inert medal. The source global/chat announcement for the implemented T-90M upgrade remains a presentation gap; its mechanical conversion and private inspection label are active.
- Ordinary vehicle acceleration, reverse and route arrival still follow native locomotion. The source decisions depend on the complete RW path/formation order policy, which has not been supplied by the direct FPV motion adapter. A post-update position correction is not used.
- Infantry squad/loadout simulation, callsigns, source helper-message presentation and the remaining sensor/flight/transport feature ledger belong to the corresponding pack modules and broader compatibility report.

## Verification

`python3 -B test_component_port.py` and `python3 -B test_logistics_port.py` use the original supplied RW files. `bun test ./extensions/combined-arms-v1/test/contentModulesCombat.test.ts` covers coupled damage, repair, morale/rank, target selection, finite supplies, queued reload continuation, source flat armor versus vanilla armor, forest effects, source crew thresholds, APS selection/reload, helper identity parity, additive and expanding HP, fragment chains, projectile motion, warmup and validated save continuation. Package TypeScript checking and the full manifest/bundle tests are separate integration checks.

`tested_supported_component_names()` produces `combat-coverage.json`. `python3 component_port.py` remains the raw source-contract audit; its `omitted` audit records describe requirements without assembled inventory and must not be confused with the final manifest activation result.
