# UCGR appearance and recorded feedback

The implementation and media live in this pack. No game source is changed by this
work. `feedback-source-data.json`, `media-report.json` and the muzzle audit carry
the source paths and exact per-definition mappings generated by `port.py`.

## Implemented source mappings

- Tank gun assemblies are split from their original pixel layers according to
  the retained weapons' independent rings and slave chains. This includes the
  MAIN/NSVT template, LAV25's second ring and BMP3's hull machine gun. Literally
  invisible or transparent launchers receive an empty pivot sheet; they do not
  duplicate visible turret art. The articulation importer must accept the full
  geometry before the exporter separates any layers.
- A turret's `image_drawOffsetX/Y` moves its image in the turret's own turning
  frame, with y forward like the turret's position. A spinning rotor blade
  keeps that offset in its own frame, so each blade's hub lands on the
  turret position (MI-4).
- Turret layers leave out the drop shadow their source paints in translucent
  near-black: the game casts each turret's shadow from its silhouette, with
  the hull's. BMP-1AM and PGZ-88 keep theirs, the only trace of a barrel the
  port does not draw: BMP-1AM's 1 px barrel falls out of its 0.95
  nearest-neighbour scale, and PGZ-88's barrels are an `onTop` decal.
- A10's independently aiming cannon uses a hull-fixed anchor and a bore-relative
  barrel offset. Native projectile emission, muzzle cycling and recoil remain
  responsible for the actual shot.
- Source weapon projectile PNGs, authored scale, explicit invisible rounds and
  landed wreck image layers are used directly. PNG bytes are copied unchanged.
  Source projectile motion supplies public height for helper and fragment art.
- Source selection, movement and attack recordings preserve silence where the
  source has no active binding. Recorded firing, jet, reload, impact and tank
  death cues are decoded to PCM16 WAV at the original rate and channel count.
  Firing cues preserve explicit `shoot_sound_vol` as a replacement for the
  recording suffix gain; absent both, RW defaults to 0.3. Alternative recordings
  cycle in source order and simulation cursors survive checkpoints. They replace the corresponding native sound event only. Native
  recoil and other sounds are retained.
- Attached radar missile recordings follow the source queued lock action:
  emission after its original delay, at the helper's then-current position,
  canceled if it no longer exists. Pending cues survive a checkpoint and
  contain no hidden target identity in filtered views.
- The jet's explicit `int(timeAlive/0.1)%20==0` gate produces one original cue
  every two seconds. Completed automatic magazine reloads use the logistics
  completion serial; service resupply does not impersonate a reload.
- Component warning/destruction images use the source inclusive thresholds and
  fixed world-axis offsets. Allied selected front, side and rear ERA bars use their four source
  frames. Public replication contains icon/frame choices, not enemy component
  damage, ammunition or hidden state.
- A failed ammunition rack chooses the source low/high branch from the sampled
  hull value once. The low branch's missing turret remains missing through
  saves and visible enemy snapshots. Supported original effect images retain
  source frame timing, scale/fade, lifetime and linear motion.
- Non-looping effect animations terminate at their authored final frame, and
  `alsoEmitEffects` does not recursively execute grandchildren which RW itself
  ignores. Literal land/liquid creation predicates gate images and sound
  together at the effect's source position. Original tank cannon smoke/dust
  retains its uniform source offset/direction/velocity ranges, draws beneath
  units and drifts independently while the native recoil marker remains intact.
- Source aircraft height is shared by the live sprite pose and native shadow
  placement. Landing state is not repurposed to store source altitude.
  Original helper missile sprites consume the source ordnance module's public
  height, using the same height projection as live aircraft.
- Recorded world cues use the package-owned recorded-audio scope, positional
  visibility, pause/resume, seek reset and disposal. Cosmetic choices do not
  consume combat randomness. Effects use the host's elapsed-age clock; decals
  use remaining life.

- Original three/five-member controllers carry the source transparent image.
  Their entrenched forms draw the original foxhole image below every member,
  with its fixed world direction and authored height offset; no host building
  footprint or fabricated body stands in for the source logical controller.

- Source-owned impact lists suppress the native class burst, crater and
  impact recording, including source `NONE`; unsupported effects remain absent.
  RW built-in projectile/impact atlases are not supplied with this pack; these
  visuals are omitted rather than substituted with native class art.

- Split batteries retain their original transport and deployed body sheets.
  HAWK's three visible missile layers follow its actual carried stock, including
  after reload and save/frame restoration. Only a visible appearance index is
  published to opposing clients. Radar sheets use the source pivot, continuously
  updated source heading while active, and the stationary source `r_1` heading
  while off; they use the normal body, ring, lighting and shadow passes.
- Multi-helper artillery impact records retain the source's expanded spawn-list
  indices. A child `NONE` impact cannot replay its parent explosion. Child
  effects that fail the same source-clause checks stay absent. AP/HEAT/TAM
  fragments use original `spark.png` at source drawSize0.3, and AP ricochets
  use their own original `rico.png` and source scale. Nested artillery helper
  indices select the matching child appearance after save and visible frames.

- FPV operators use separate original ground sheets for the operator/trench and
  original vehicle body sheets above them. Deployed poses keep their authored
  fixed world direction. Russian vehicles retain all three original UAZ images
  selected by the source `self.id % 3` expression.
- Source atmospheric/physics particles have a pack-owned state and renderer.
  Original smoke keeps its detached ten-second visual life, half-second fade-in,
  original scale/alpha/random drift and visibility through fog. Filtered snapshots
  expose the public particle pose without its hidden emitter's state. Source
  fade-in transitions into fade-out at full alpha instead of multiplying two ramps.

## Exact remaining source uncertainty / omissions

These are not silently represented by invented parameters. They remain pack
work or require evidence of Rusted Warfare's precise behavior; they do not
justify a game-code change.

- The four source squad controllers still appear in native minimap/tactical
  glyph layers. Those HUD layers have no pack renderer hook and bypass the
  extension visibility filter for allied entities; world body/icon drawing is
  suppressed. No game code or HUD monkey patch was used.
- BMP2M’s AGS source firing gain is 5. The recorded-audio API accepts 0..4,
  so that cue is omitted without clamping; it has no synthesized replacement.
- Tank movement audio is tied to `movementEffectRate:5`, while the source
  recording lasts longer than a putative five-tick interval. The source's
  playback overlap/deduplication semantics are not established; no replacement
  loop or guessed throttle is invented.
- Projected image shadows, built-in RW atlases, color multiplication,
  randomized animation/lifetimes and effect death chains are not yet reproduced.
  Live-parent particle translation remains omitted where its source attachment
  requests it. Every encountered unsupported effect clause is recorded in
  `remainingEffectClauses`. Source countermeasure emissions and the original
  missile trail still need bindings to their runtime emission clocks.
  Native smoke trails remain suppressed for source-owned projectile art.
- Client camera-dependent effect creation (`createWhenOffscreen` and
  `createWhenZoomedOut`) is not replicated; supported particles retain native
  fog filtering. The original cannon smoke explicitly permits offscreen creation.
- Source-specific falling wreck trajectories, airborne death effects and
  wreck persistence still differ from the host's native crash lifecycle.
  Landed original wreck art alone does not claim to close this behavior gap.
- Full-spectrum-mode music and opening rules belong to the explicitly skipped
  M5 mode. They are not activated as unconditional national-faction music.

## Verification

`test_media.py` compares complete source-layer inventories before/after automatic
splitting across the selected roster. `test_articulation_port.py` additionally
compares pixel contents and world pivots. `test_muzzle_port.py` and
`muzzle-game.test.ts` exercise source muzzle conversion and emitted shots.

`test_feedback_port.py` checks source PNG/decoded PCM equality, explicit gains,
visibility predicates, ERA thresholds and source drift rejection.
`feedback-game.test.ts` exercises actual pack saves, fog-filtered full/delta
frames, reload/jet clocks, rack state, effect lifetime, audio ownership and live
aircraft pose. `appearance.test.ts` verifies deployment/stock selection and public
state redaction; `test_media.py` checks every authored battery body/missile layer. Run these after regenerating and bundling the current pack.


## Pinned engine evidence

The official field table documents atmospheric drag/wind, fog visibility and
physics fields but omits several numeric defaults. These are now checked against
[the RW 1.15 decompilation](https://github.com/coolomet/RustedWarfare/tree/52758091b7888bbe4797789d59952f34653ac960),
pinned to commit `52758091b7888bbe4797789d59952f34653ac960`.
Its README identifies version 1.15 and Fernflower, and reports checksum divergence
when recompiling multiplayer/replay battles. This is engineering evidence for the
specific inspected algorithms, not a claim that this source tree was rebuilt or
validated against the exact original mod author's executable.

- `class_436.ea` initializes turret size from `attack.turretSize` (default 1),
  multiplied by `core.globalScale` (default 1). `class_397.X` inherits it;
  explicit `barrelY`/`size` replaces it. This resolves EC556HAD's three guns,
  MI4's gun and both UH1 guns without using collision radii.
- `class_393.b` initializes recording gain to 0.3. `class_392.a(float)` replaces
  every cue gain and its playback method advances a cyclic list; `class_397`
  invokes that replacement for explicit `shoot_sound_vol`.
- `class_429` defaults `liveAfterAttachedDies` to true. Surviving impact particles
  do not require a redundant explicit declaration in the mod.
- `class_998.b(float)` reduces height velocity by `R * .002 * dtTicks` for
  atmosphere and adds absolute-X wind `-.0015 * dtTicks`. Physics applies
  `-.1 * gravity * dtTicks` to height velocity above ground; ground contact
  reverses and halves downward speed, approaches it toward zero by 1.3,
  and damps XY velocity by `.15 * dtTicks` and spin by `1 * dtTicks`.
  The pack translates ticks and coordinates without adding planar atmospheric
  drag. `sourceParticles.test.ts` verifies the numeric transition, fog privacy,
  lifetime, checkpoint continuation and sound-list continuation.

The movement implementation has a separate owner and source audit; this document
does not claim that ordinary host movement itself provides RW kinematic parity.
