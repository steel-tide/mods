# Adolence: Original Maps II

Eight native layout ports from the maps bundled with Adolence: The Long Bridge,
Industrial Zone Mini, Great City, Crowded City, Dead City, Forest Lake, Forest
Lake Remastered and The Opposite Bank.

Enable this pack alongside **Adolence: City of Sin** for the complete collection
of 15 originals plus the City of Sin showcase, and select Adolence factions in
match setup. This pack also works on its own with vanilla factions. Steel Tide
limits each mod to eight maps, so the collection is split across two packs.
Requires Steel Tide 0.8.16.

The maps use native terrain and a grey urban material selected only by these
maps. Starts are capped to eight. Great City is reduced from 1000×1000 to
500×500. Crowded City and Dead City share their original terrain and are
**skirmish adaptations**; RW survival scripts and crowds are not included.

See [the complete inventory and conversion notes](../adolence/MAPS.md) for
source filenames, player counts, limitations and regeneration instructions.
The converter lives in `../adolence/port-maps.py`; no game code is required.

Original layouts are from Adolence Q3.6.0 by 自然常数也有人用? and the Adolence
team, under `LicenseRef-Adolence` and the original port’s author permission.
Industrial Zone Mini is credited to SeCtAnT ft. Teoshebo in its source filename.
