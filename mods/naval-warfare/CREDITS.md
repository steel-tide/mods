# Credits and source provenance

Original title, verbatim:

> NW (NAVAL WARFARE)0.10.1J(UNBalance)The conventional version

Source: the user-supplied `rusted-warfare-mods/naval-warfare` directory.
`source-inventory.json` records every INI path and its SHA-256 fingerprint;
`conversion-report.json` maps exported pictures and sounds back to source
paths and records omitted units. The embedded archive names and their map
dimensions are documented in `COMPATIBILITY.md`.

The source's `mod-info.txt` supplies the title, Japanese description and
music directory, but **does not name the original author or state a license**.
No attribution to a guessed creator, blanket Creative Commons grant, or
permission to republish the soundtrack is asserted. Images, music, sounds,
historical prose and unit designs retain their respective original owners.

The Steel Tide conversion, translations, manifest, package implementation,
test fixtures and Naval Review map were prepared for this local port.
The non-executing RW parser is adapted from this repository's existing
`mods/usgr-re/rw.py`; its asset conversion and extension are owned by this
pack and do not depend on that draft at runtime.

Steel Tide belongs to its respective rights holders. The port uses the
original Naval Warfare Excavator and crane pictures. No game implementation
or native engineer image is bundled in the extension modules or assets.
